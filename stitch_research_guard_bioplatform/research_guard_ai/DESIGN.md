## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-scientific environment engineered for advanced biomedical intelligence and scientific validation. The aesthetic bridges the authority of traditional academic publishing with the precision of next-generation computational biology.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Rigorous & Authoritative:** The interface conveys institutional credibility, peer-reviewed precision, and data integrity.
- **Bioluminescent & Intelligent:** Controlled emerald luminescence signifies active AI surveillance, sequence validation, and autonomous synthesis.
- **Focused & Calm:** Deep forest slate teal surfaces eliminate visual fatigue during exhaustive sequence analysis and analytical reviews.

### Design Movement: Scientific Neo-Editorial Glass
The design system synthesizes **Editorial Clarity** and **Instrumental Glassmorphism**:
- Translucent pine and teal-tinted panels (`rgba(15, 36, 36, 0.7)`) layered over deep slate canvases.
- Micro-hairline structural boundaries mimicking precision optical instrumentation and laboratory microfluidic chips.
- Academic serif titles juxtaposed against crisp technical sans-serif body data and monospaced telemetry tags.

## Colors

The palette balances deep oceanic pine foundations with energetic bioluminescent indicators and strict epistemic validation accents.

### Color Tiers & Semantic Architecture
- **Primary (`#10B981`, `#059669`, `#34D399`):** Bioluminescent emerald. Reserved for validated states, AI operational certainty, safe sequence alignments, active telemetry, and high-confidence metrics.
- **Secondary (`#0F2424`):** Forest Slate Teal. Forms elevated containers, floating scientific consoles, modal planes, and header architectures.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#081717`):** Abyssal Pine Slate. Base background for full viewport immersion; absorbs ambient glare and drives visual focus inward.
- **Light Contrast Variant / Print Substrates (`#F4F7F4`, `#EDF2EE`):** Warm pale sage. Used for exported manuscript previews, regulatory dossiers, and high-legibility document overlays.
- **Tertiary / Warning (`#F59E0B`, `#D97706`):** Amber gold. Explicitly indicates verification locks, access thresholds, peer dispute states, and biohazard classification flags.
- **Critical / Danger (`#E11D48`):** Muted crimson. Denotes protocol violations, synthetic replication anomalies, or compromised chain-of-custody data.

### Interaction & Glass Application
- Surface boundaries use `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` for ambient bioluminescent edges.
- Interactive states shift from static slate teal into glowing emerald-tinted translucent surfaces (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces strict role separation between academic prestige, analytical legibility, and technical instrumentation:

- **Editorial Headings (`Newsreader`):** Infuses centuries of peer-reviewed editorial authority into synthetic biological findings and executive research syntheses. Set with relaxed, natural leading and slight negative tracking.
- **Analytical Body (`Inter`):** Delivers clean optical rhythm across high-density research findings, abstracts, hypothesis rationales, and analytical tables.
- **Instrumental Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated exclusively to DOIs, GenBank accession codes, chemical registry numbers, base-pair counts, verification hashes, and cryptographic signatures. All lowercase numerical tokens must align via tabular figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by balanced laboratory workstation layouts.

### Structural Framework
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column grid with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Accommodates side-by-side molecular inspector panes, active telemetry logs, and editorial review columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Analytical tools collapse into swipeable, segmented panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem`. Complex molecular graphs collapse into sequential cards with modal drill-downs.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing follows an 8pt base grid for component placement and visual rhythm. High-density data matrices adopt `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compactness, while clinical manuscript viewports rely on `space-lg` and `space-xl` to preserve editorial breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous glass layering and tinted spatial depth, rejecting generic grey shadows in favor of deep atmospheric teal tones.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas (Level 0):** Pure `#081717`. The bedrock substrate.
- **Base Console (Level 1):** Solid `#0F2424` with a 1px border rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Raised Analytical Card (Level 2):** Translucent backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(15, 36, 36, 0.75)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, ambient shadow `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(4, 12, 12, 0.5)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Diagnostics (Level 3):** Background `rgba(18, 44, 44, 0.9)`, border `1px solid rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.25)`, glowing drop shadow `0 12px 40px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 20px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.

### Optical Accents & Bioluminescent Sheen
Active nodes and interactive focus panels project a subtle 1px radial inner highlight along the top border (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.3)`), evoking laboratory glass exposed to pinpoint light emitters.

## Shapes

The design system employs precise, low-radius curvature (`roundedness: 1`, base `0.25rem` / 4px) to retain an instrumental, medical-grade feel. 

### Corner Archetypes
- **Interactive Controls & Inputs:** 4px radius (`0.25rem`). Maintains the crisp edge profile of precision equipment interfaces.
- **Glass Analytical Panels & Viewports:** 8px radius (`0.5rem`). Softens high-volume data grids without yielding technical authority.
- **Status Pills, Tags, and Access Tokens:** Fully rounded pill-capsules (`9999px`) reserved strictly for atomic biological markers, status indicators, and cryptographic DOI badges to contrast cleanly with structural grid cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Validated / Synthesis):** Solid bioluminescent fill `#10B981` text `#081717` (bold `Inter`), 4px corner radius. On hover, background shifts to `#34D399` with an emerald edge glow (`0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action (Console Command):** Translucent background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`, text `#34D399`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Restricted / Warning Action:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, text `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

### Scientific Tags & Chips
- Designed with `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` uppercase.
- Contained within 9999px pill bounds with a 1px border. 
- Validation tag uses `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` background with `#34D399` text. Warning/Quarantine tags use `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)` background with `#F59E0B` text. Includes an 8px circular glowing LED indicator at the leading edge.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `rgba(8, 23, 23, 0.8)`. Border: 1px hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Radius: 4px.
- Typography: `Inter` for search phrases; automatic switch to `JetBrains Mono` when raw nucleic acid strings, SMILES structures, or hash queries are detected.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#10B981` with ambient internal glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).

### Analytical Lists & Data Records
- Structured with zebra styling using alternating fills of `transparent` and `rgba(15, 36, 36, 0.35)`.
- Row dividers set to hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- Metadata cells render in tabular `JetBrains Mono` aligned flush right; scientific descriptions render in `Inter` aligned flush left.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px boxes with 2px radius. Inactive: hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on deep slate.
- Checked state: `#10B981` background featuring a crisp dark slate checkmark icon and emerald outer bloom.

### Analytical Dossier Cards
- Built using Level 2 elevation glass with hairline borders.
- Card headers feature an editorial `Newsreader` headline accompanied by a monospaced accession badge anchored in the top-right corner.
- Bottom actions feature subtle 1px divider lines with contextual status telemetry.

### Molecular Telemetry Banner (Domain Specific)
- Full-width translucent alert console fixed to sequence viewing canvases.
- Displays nucleotide alignment accuracy, real-time cryptographic audit trails, and autonomous guard agent activity using amber and emerald bioluminescent pulses.
