#01 Mechanistic Pathway Assertion
Partially Supported — Requires Qualification
Original AI-Generated Claim

“MRTX1133 induces rapid downregulation of p-ERK followed by rebound activation at 72 hours via EGFR-dependent signaling.”

biotech Direct Observation

Western blot immunoblots confirm phosphorylated ERK1/2 levels attenuate at 2h post-exposure, followed by a measurable band intensity surge at 72h.
